Good to know

Simple terms, so you know exactly how your sessions work before you book.

Do my sessions expire?

One to three sessions never expire, so you can book whenever you’re ready. You can purchase a package of four to eight sessions whenever you like; its two-month window begins with your first session.

How long do I have to use a pack?

Packs of 4 to 8 sessions can spread out over up to two months, starting from your first session. The 5, 6, and 7-session options work as add-ons inside that same two-month window.

When can we train?

Saturdays and Sundays are the most open. Weekday sessions run after 5 PM.
